This is a dummy entry, added to check that a single new post shows up without surprises in a site with ten thousand others.

## Why add one post

A large content set hides small regressions. Adding one entry at a time is the cheapest way to see what a change actually costs.

## What to look for

The post should appear first on the blog index, on its author page, and under its category. Nothing else should change.
